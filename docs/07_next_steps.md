## Downstream

### Phyloseq

Integrate the ASV table, taxonomy, and sample metadata.

### Alpha diversity

Evaluate appropriate measures such as observed ASVs and Shannon diversity.

### Beta diversity

Assess community dissimilarity and visualize community structure using appropriate ordination methods.

### PERMANOVA

Test genotype, developmental stage, and spatial compartment effects using a model justified by the actual sample design and replication.

### Variance partitioning

Estimate unique and shared contributions of explanatory variables where the design supports the analysis.

### Predictive modelling

Random Forest is proposed for prediction of genotype, developmental stage, and spatial compartment. Model complexity and validation must be selected based on the final independent sample size and metadata structure.
